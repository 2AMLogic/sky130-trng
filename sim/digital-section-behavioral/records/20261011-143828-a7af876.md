## Notes

- RCT/APT are *bias and run-length* detectors. A balanced period-k pattern has marginal bias 0.5 and run
  lengths bounded by k, so it is invisible to both regardless of how little entropy it carries; this is the
  expected, documented limit of SP 800-90B §4.4, quantified here, not a defect in the model.
- The estimators are offline (N >= 1000-12000 samples, run over a whole stream); they are not an online
  mitigation. Where they catch a case the online tests miss, the gap is in *what runs on-chip*.
- Estimator point estimates can over- or under-state `h_true`; 'catches' uses the design target as the
  threshold and is a detection statement, not an accuracy claim.
- Lock-mix streams are non-stationary, which the 90B estimators assume away: their flags on
  streams whose analytic `h_true` is at/above target reflect the repeated locked segments (a
  conservative reading), not an error in the analytic figure under the stated known-schedule model.
- Ratified cutoffs and spec text are unchanged. Any coverage hole is routed to a decision-record request.
