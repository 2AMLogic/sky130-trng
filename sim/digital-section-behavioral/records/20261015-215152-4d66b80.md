## False-alarm check on seeded i.i.d. streams

Analytic limit: per lag `2^-40 / L`, per window `<= 2^-40` for any i.i.d. source with min-entropy >= `H_DESIGN` (union bound; the lag counts are positively correlated, so this is conservative).
