First divergence cycle (RTL vs model): 185 of 4239; gate vs model: 185; the mapped netlist reproduces the variant RTL trace bit-for-bit. The failing phases are exactly the depth-observable ones: the first mismatch is a STATUS read whose RAW_LEVEL field saturates at 2 instead of 4, and every later phase that reads the FIFOs or STATUS inherits the occupancy difference; the phases that never observe a full FIFO match the model. The variant is intentionally NOT sequentially equivalent to the normative model at its default depth 4 (observable FIFO depth), so `klt equiv` is not run against it. Against the normative model's OWN `fifo_depth=2` parameterisation (`TrngDigital(fifo_depth=2)` -- the model already parameterises depth; nothing in `digital/model/` is modified) the variant RTL MATCHES the full 4239-cycle directed program (0 mismatches), and the mapped netlist matches too. Adoption is a DR-0004 section 4.4 change: depth 4 -> 2, STATUS level-field semantics saturate at 2, overflow trips two words earlier, and the normative model plus the directed programs would pin the same depth parameter before any equivalence chain could be re-established (tracked in #610).

## Item 4 -- combined options

No combination of the studied options reaches 0.05 mm2. The best is 0.060740 mm2 (1.215x target), short by 0.010740 mm2. Holding the F1 width (218.675 um), the whole-block height budget for 0.05 mm2 leaves a digital macro at most 116.6 um tall -- vs the 165.7 um the FIFO-2 variant already estimates, so the target needs a digital section roughly half today's cell area AND an analog row narrower than F1, not a floorplan change.
