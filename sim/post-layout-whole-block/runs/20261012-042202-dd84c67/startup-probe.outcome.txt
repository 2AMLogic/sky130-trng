startup-probe (local, single corner tt / 27 C / 1.80 V, 970 ns, 6 program cycles, save_mode netlist)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Run on the shared 8-vCPU host with `--backend local` (the one single-corner debug probe the host
rules allow), absolute -o path, options.timeout_s 3600.

Observed: ngspice RSS grew steadily (9.5 GB at 10 min, 12.1 GB at 20 min, 14.0 GB at 30 min,
15.8 GB at 40 min, 17.3 GB at 50 min) and ngspice.log never advanced past its header
("Circuit: * klt sim -- generated corner deck, do not edit"); no analysis banner, no measurement.
The wrapper was stopped at the ~60 minute background limit before klt wrote a report
(report.json is empty/not JSON on purpose: it is the artifact of an unfinished run, kept as the
evidence). The orphaned ngspice process was killed afterwards. An earlier attempt (relative -o,
klayout-tools#2892/#2939) and a first absolute-path attempt with the default options.timeout_s
(120 s, 'timeout' diagnostic) preceded it and are not kept.

Consequence: a 7.87 us, 75-cycle transistor-level run of the ~25 000-transistor flat netlist is
not feasible locally, and the 970 ns probe gives no rate to extrapolate from.
