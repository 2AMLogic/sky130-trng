## Measured range and recommendation (provisional)

- Measured combined standby range over the grid: **0.001517 uW** (ss/-40C/1.62V) to **38.04 uW** (ff/125C/1.98V), Liberty-derived digital term.
- **Recommended idle-target value (provisional, recommendation only): <= 200 uW**, = 1.5x the worst combined estimate (including the 125 C digital extrapolation) rounded up to the next 1-2-5 value. The margin is a stated choice, not derived. It is a worst-corner ENVELOPE (ff / 125 C / 1.98 V), where the digital term is a labelled extrapolation beyond the library's hottest view; the typical point (tt / 27 C / 1.8 V) is orders of magnitude lower (see the table), so a typical-condition idle figure is a separate choice for the decision record.
- This record makes **no** claim that any README target holds for idle. README.md:75 reads "< 500 uW active; idle target set after a sky130 leakage survey": the idle target is unset, and the 500 uW active figure is not an idle limit. It is context only: the worst combined standby estimate is a small fraction of it, but that is not a comparison against an idle requirement.
- Any adoption of the recommended value, or any violation of an existing budget, goes to a separate decision-record issue; no `README.md` / `spec/` edit is made here.

## What this record does and does not establish

- Establishes: pre-layout `.op` standby supply current of the assembled analog block with its enable low, per PVT corner, and a combined estimate with the library's digital leakage.
- Does not establish: any silicon figure; post-layout leakage (parasitic and well/substrate leakage not in the pre-layout netlist); a worst-state digital figure; leakage at 125 C from the Liberty library (no such view); the idle current of any other state (e.g. rst_n low, clk toggling). fs/sf corners and Monte Carlo are out of scope.
- Simulation-derived; provisional until measured on silicon.
