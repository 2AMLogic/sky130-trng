## Lever S: sample-before-combine, and S+J

One `sampler_dff` per ring (the `ring_bit` taps `sampler_core.spice` already
instantiate), XOR taken digitally after sampling: `N_max_combine` no longer
applies and `N` is set by the entropy law alone. The rows that bind become
power, area, idle and lock proximity. Per-ring active current is now
**measured per ring choice** (`sc3`/`sc5` worst point: ring5 33.49 uA at [125.0, 1.98, 'ff'], ring3 42.61 uA at [125.0, 1.98, 'ff']).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S (ring5) | 78 | 1 Mbps | 5.17 mW (10.3x row) | 0.262 mm2 (5.25x row) | 187 uW (264 uW x-T) | ~1173 (unspaced), 77 adjacent by construction |
| S+J (ring3) | 20 | 1 Mbps | 1.69 mW (3.4x row) | 0.056 mm2 (1.11x row) | 70 uW (147 uW x-T) | ~74 (unspaced), 19 adjacent by construction |

- **Power row**: the worst measured per-ring current permits `N_power` = 7 rings (ring5) / 5 (ring3) under the 500 uW row (array-level anchor at N = 4, ff / 125 C / 1.98 V: 473.9 uW, DR-0010 note R3). Sampler and digital-parity dynamic power at 1 MHz are negligible next to the rings (post-layout `sampler_dff` `i_act` ~ 1 uA over a ~45 ns two-capture window ~ 2.4e-14 J/capture ~ 24 nW each at 1 MHz).
- **Area row**: at macro density (73.1 um2/device from the sampler_core macro) the row permits `N_area` = 14 (ring5) / 18 (ring3); device-count floors (6.67 um2/device raw x 3-10) bracket the same conclusion.
- **Idle row**: worst-corner standby scaled from the committed survey (per stopped ring 0.255 uA, per DFF 0.762 uA at ff / 125 C / 1.98 V, digital 27.0 uW measured-view / 103.8 uW extrapolated) -- within the provisional 200 uW envelope on the measured-view basis, over it once the 125 C digital extrapolation is counted. A derived scaling, labelled as such.
- **Lock proximity is structural at these N**: inside the characterized wstv span (1.1686x period span) rings can be spaced >= 2% apart only up to `N` = 8; at N = 78 adjacent mean spacing is 0.20% -- every adjacent pair sits inside the 2% lock-proximity scale of the committed mismatch model (which already draws min-pair < 2% in 14% of its N = 4 draws). Trim/selection is #221's open question; nothing here assumes it.

### New failure modes of sample-before-combine (stated per acceptance criterion)

- **Per-sampler metastability**: each ring is sampled asynchronously at 1 MHz against a 1/T_0 ~ 0.4-2.6 GHz (ring3) / 0.2-0.6 GHz (ring5, loaded) node. Post-layout `tcq` is 0.18-0.30 ns (`sim/post-layout-sampler-dff/`); no committed record measures the metastability resolution time constant or MTBF -- **unmeasured**. The mechanism is not new (today's single raw tap is already an async sample of `xo`); its multiplicity scales xN.
- **Per-sampler offset**: input-referred offset sigma 16.0 mV, range -46..+40 mV (`sim/local-mismatch-monte-carlo/`). Independent per-sampler offsets (and independent per-ring duty biases) attenuate in the digital parity as |1-2p|^N: at the committed sampler-mismatch-only duty spread (p = 0.538-0.544) the parity bias at N = 11-78 is < 1e-7 -- arithmetic, not a measurement. **A common-mode duty term driven by a shared clock edge or a common ring-duty systematic is unmeasured**; the committed duty evidence is at `xo` (post-XOR), where per-ring duties already cancel once. The `sc3`/`sc5` decks now measure per-ring duty directly (duty_ring in the committed method); it is recorded in the raw responses and left for #221's analysis.
- **Correlated sampling instants**: one shared `clk` means sample-instant jitter enters every ring's sampled phase as a common-mode term (+/- sigma_clk/T_0,i of a period per ring); the entropy law credits each ring only its own accumulated jitter. The derate is **unmeasured** -- no committed record characterizes the sample clock's jitter (it is an off-block input).
- **Ring-to-ring lock proximity**: structural at N >= ~9 inside the characterized wstv span (above); at the N the entropy law asks for, re-spacing alone cannot fix it.

## The three rows together, and the conclusion

| option | rate row (> 1 Mbps) | power row (< 500 uW) | area row (< 0.05 mm2) | ceiling with every row held |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| committed (N = 4, analog XOR) | unmet (50 kbps; ceiling 77.7 kbps, combining-bound) | met (474 uW array worst) | unmet (whole block 0.126 mm2) | 77.7 kbps |
| J only (ring3, analog XOR) | unmet (ceiling 104 kbps, combining-bound; swing below DR-0002's bar everywhere) | met at N_max = 2 | as committed | 104 kbps |
| S only (ring5) | **met** at N = 78 | **broken** (5.2 mW, 10.3x) | **broken** (0.262 mm2 macro-density est) | 91 kbps (N = 7) |
| S+J (ring3) | **met** at N = 20 | **broken** (1.69 mW, 3.4x) | broken (0.056 mm2 est) | **260 kbps** (N = 5, power-bound) |

**Negative result.** Sample-before-combine does remove `N_max_combine` -- that half of the hypothesis is confirmed -- and the 3-inversion ring measures **4.0x** ring5's per-ring Q at the entropy-binding corner, cutting `N` at 1 Mbps from 78 to 20. But 1 Mbps still cannot be met with the other rows held: even S+J needs 1.69 mW of ring supply current alone. **The limiting quantity is the per-ring active supply current at the worst measured corner (ff / 125 C / 1.98 V) against the < 500 uW row**; area and lock proximity bind within a factor of ~2 of it (N_area = 18, N_lock-free = 8). The best reachable rate with every row held is **~260 kbps** (S+J at N = 5) against the committed topology's 78 kbps combining-bound ceiling -- a real improvement, still well short of 1 Mbps, and riding a cell whose ring-node swing misses DR-0002's 0.80 bar at every point measured and nearly fails to oscillate at 125 C / 1.98 V. The conclusion is robust to the estimator's statistics: even a +50 % (~3 sigma) favourable `sigma_1` draw moves the all-rows ceiling only to ~585 kbps (N@1 Mbps = 9). A higher rate bought by breaking the power or area row is exactly that; this record does not propose it.

Every entropy figure here is simulation-derived and provisional until silicon; every `sigma_1` is a single ~16 %-1sigma draw on top of DR-0002 Sec 6's ~1.5-2x injected-level systematic, so every N and rate ceiling inherits both.
