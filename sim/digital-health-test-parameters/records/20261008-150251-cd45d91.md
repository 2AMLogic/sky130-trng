**Caveats that bound every conclusion below.** The streams come from
a behavioural model with independent noise increments, not from
silicon; estimated `H` is a model number and is **provisional until
measured on silicon**. A replay with zero alarms on such data is weak
evidence for the real device, and `H` = 0.5 versus 0.65 on the model
does not exercise real-world bias, correlation or supply coupling.
The cited battery record reports single-sequence FAILs on most of
these streams (listed in section 2) -- the estimated `H` and the
replay must be read alongside them, not instead of them.

## 1. Cutoffs re-derived at the estimated H

`C_RCT` = 81 and `C_APT` = 824 are the ratified
values at `H` = 0.5. Verdict rule: **violated** if
`H_est` < 0.5 (the cutoffs would under-alarm), **tight** if
0.5 <= `H_est` < 0.55, otherwise
**conservative** (false alarms rarer than designed). Lookups are
`params.c_rct(H)` / `params.c_apt(H)`, not retyped.

cutoff if the source really had `H_est` and were i.i.d. at

The sensitivity paragraph below uses `-log2(C_APT / W)`, the
min-entropy of an i.i.d. biased source whose *typical* window just

## 3. Failure injection and detection latency

Healthy base: each of the 18 streams above. A failure of
16384 samples is spliced in at onsets [2000, 3333, 5117, 7001, 9500, 11777] (none a
multiple of 1024); the injector replaces the healthy samples, same
stream length, deterministic via per-scenario CRC32 seeds. Each of the
108 runs per scenario feeds RCT and APT
(ratified cutoffs, individual tests, restart after a trip) from sample
0 and records the first trip at or after onset (a stuck run can trip
RCT a few samples early when the preceding healthy sample already
equals the stuck value). Latency = injected
samples consumed up to and including the failing one; seconds at
50,000 bps (20 us/sample). APT latency is quantised to
1024-sample window ends measured from stream start, so it depends on
onset alignment. 'Not detected' is a result, not an omission.

Reading the table: RCT is the only test that catches a stuck source
quickly (81 samples = 1.62 ms) and cannot see any
toggling or bias that keeps runs short. APT needs a window whose
reference value fills >= 824/1024 = 0.805 of the window, so a
fixed bias below roughly p = 0.8 -- including p = 0.7, the 'plausible
drift' value, which still carries 0.515 bit/sample -- is **not** flagged at the ratified
cutoff, and a period-3 or period-4 pattern with <= 75% of one symbol is
invisible to both tests. Those are properties of the SP 800-90B
continuous tests at this alpha, not defects of the model; the
statistical battery and the estimators, not RCT/APT, are what cover
them.
