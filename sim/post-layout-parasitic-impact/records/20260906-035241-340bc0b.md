**Coupling-attributable period shift: at most 0.0330% of the ring period**, anywhere on the grid, at the pessimistic (infinite-impedance shared substrate) bound.

The transient solver's own numerical period scatter, measured with no injected noise at the same 5 ps timestep, is **0.0241% of T_0** (`sim/ro-ring-timestep-convergence/records/20260825-053701-54f5715`). 1 of 12 grid points exceed it, and the shift's sign is NOT consistent across the grid.

So the coupling shift is an **upper bound, not a resolved measurement**. A real frequency pull would move every ring the same way; this one does not clear the solver's own period scatter at most points and does not keep a consistent sign. What IS resolved is that the shared node genuinely carries all four rings' activity -- it swings 3.79% of Vdd peak to peak with four rings running against 1.11% with one -- and that even so, the frequency pull it produces stays under 0.033% of the ring period.

The floating node's *loading* effect, which the solo control separates out, is -0.151% to -0.057% -- an artifact of the unmodelled substrate tie, not a statement about ring independence.
