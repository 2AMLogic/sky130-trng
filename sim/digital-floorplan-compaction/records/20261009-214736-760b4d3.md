## Recommendation

**Floorplan compaction alone cannot close the area target.** No measured point gets even the re-stacked estimate under 0.05 mm2. The smallest is `util65` at 0.093609 mm2, 1.87x the target, removing 43 % of the gap. The floors above show why: the analog row plus the standard cells exceed the target before any utilisation choice is made.

**No candidate is selected.** The committed 40 % baseline remains the only selectable digital geometry. This is why:

- `util55` matches the baseline on route DRC, antenna, klt DRC, LVS (signal and power), 16-corner setup/hold, cosim and all four controls. It increases the unrepaired library max-slew / max-cap violations (slew/cap, run vs baseline) at: ss_n40C_1v28 315/9 vs 297/10; ss_n40C_1v60 581/16 vs 536/18; ss_n40C_1v76 107/2 vs 104/2 (16-corner total 1165 vs 1167). Those are electrical design-rule violations, so this is a degraded layout and is not promoted.
- `util65` matches the baseline on route DRC, antenna, klt DRC, LVS (signal and power), 16-corner setup/hold, cosim and all four controls. It increases the unrepaired library max-slew / max-cap violations (slew/cap, run vs baseline) at: ss_100C_1v60 213/7 vs 193/7; ss_n40C_1v28 331/11 vs 297/10; ss_n40C_1v60 545/16 vs 536/18; ss_n40C_1v76 144/4 vs 104/2 (16-corner total 1271 vs 1167). Those are electrical design-rule violations, so this is a degraded layout and is not promoted.

Even an unconditionally accepted compacted point would leave the block at 1.87x the target. That is too little to justify spending a composition and re-verification cycle on utilisation alone.

Residual gap: +0.076116 mm2 for the committed layout (verified, 0.126116 mm2). The best measured compaction (`util65`, estimate B, not composed) would still be +0.043609 mm2. Closing the gap needs changes this study deliberately does not make. They are listed below as separate proposals, not filed by this change:

1. Analog footprint / topology: the 330.96 um-wide single-row `sampler_core` sets the full-width analog row, which is 0.029273 mm2 with its gap and pad row. A folded (multi-row) ring/sampler arrangement or a side-by-side composition moves the floor more than any digital utilisation.
2. Digital logic area: the standard cells alone are 46 % of the target. FIFO depth / storage style is the obvious candidate. It carries functional risk and needs its own spec / decision record.
3. Composition overhead: the inter-macro lane corridor, VPWR west escape and pad row; met3/met4 routing roles (tool gap already recorded in `layout/trng_whole/README.md`) would allow lanes over the macros.
4. Digital aspect ratio matched to the analog width (held at 1.0 here). This only helps together with 1 or 2.
5. Library-limit repair: the request's `constraints.max_transition_ns` / `max_capacitance_pf`, which the pinned klt build accepts, could target the unrepaired low-voltage ss-corner slew/cap violations that disqualify the compacted points. Every point, the baseline included, carries them. Changing constraints is outside this fixed-constraint study.
