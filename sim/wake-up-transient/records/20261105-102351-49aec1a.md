**Not covered by the start-up gate**: the raw path is never gated (DR-0004 sec. 4), so raw words read in the first ~47 samples after a wake-up (worst corner) carry a per-index marginal bias above 2^-10 across wake-ups. This does not change the per-sample conditional entropy the sizing law accounts for (the jitter added per sample is the same as in steady state; a deterministic start is the case where the previous phase is exactly known), but a consumer of the raw path who wants stationary bits should discard the start-up window too.
