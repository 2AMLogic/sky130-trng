## Margin statement (DR-0004 start-up window, 1024 samples = 20.48 ms at 50 kbps)

   - enable-gated wake: every ring within 1 % of its final period by **2.61 ns** after the enable (worst at -40 C / 1.62 V, `20261009-093152-e57ad27`) = 0.0131 % of one 20 us sample interval; the start-up window is 7.85e+06x longer.
   - supply-ramp wake (1 us ramp, enables tied to the rail): settled by **3.99 ns** after the ramp ends (`20261009-093152-e57ad27`); window margin 5.14e+06x. A slower ramp is quasi-static for a ring with a few-ns period: the ring tracks the rail, so the settling after the rail is final is not longer than this.
   - so the analog source is oscillating at its steady frequency before the FIRST 50 kbps sample: the start-up test does not see an un-started or still-accelerating oscillator.
