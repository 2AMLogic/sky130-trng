## What this record does and does not establish

- It **does** establish that the committed RTL implements the
  committed behavioural model over the programmed stimulus, including
  the ordering the model's cycle contract fixes (sample before bus
  access, register pop beating a streaming pop on the same FIFO).
- It is **not** exhaustive: it is a directed program, not a formal
  equivalence proof or a constrained-random campaign. The phase table
  above is the honest statement of what was exercised.
- It is **RTL simulation only** -- no synthesis, no `sky130_fd_sc_hd`
  mapping, no timing. There is therefore no `Fmax`, area or power
  claim here, and none should be read into it.
