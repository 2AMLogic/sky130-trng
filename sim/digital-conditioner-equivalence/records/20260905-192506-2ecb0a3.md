## What this record does and does not establish

- It **does** establish that the committed conditioner computes CRC-32
  as specified, that each output word depends on exactly its own 256
  raw bits, and that a flush leaves no residue across a gate or a mode
  switch.
- Check 4 is a **sanity floor, not an entropy claim.** A CRC is a
  linear map over GF(2); it redistributes entropy, it cannot create
  any. Per SP 800-90B this is a *non-vetted* conditioner, so the
  block's entropy accounting is stated at the raw tap and never at the
  conditioner output (DR-0004 §3.3).
- It says nothing about the *entropy* of the input bits: the stimulus
  is a declared synthetic pseudorandom source, and the real one has
  not been simulated (issue #21).
